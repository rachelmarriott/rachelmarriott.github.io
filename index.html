I am a Ph.D. student in Political Science at Florida State University. My research focuses on political violence, women, peace and security, conflict-related sexual violence, peace agreements, civilian protection, and gender-inclusive post-conflict institutions.

I received my B.S. in Political Science and B.S. in French from the Rochester Institute of Technology. My current research examines how wartime violence shapes the design of peace agreements and how women's political participation influences post-conflict institutional outcomes.

Research Interests

  • Political Violence
  • Women, Peace and Security
  • Conflict-Related Sexual Violence
  • Peace Agreements and Peacebuilding
  • Civilian Protection
  • Comparative Politics

Current Projects

  • Violence, Legitimacy, and Bargaining: Addressing Wartime Sexual Violence in Peace Agreement Design
  • Power Beyond Presence: Women's Civil Society Participation and Gender Provisions in Peace Agreements
  • Feminist Security Policy and Civilian Protection in Contemporary Conflict
