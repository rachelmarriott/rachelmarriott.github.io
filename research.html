Research

My research lies at the intersection of comparative politics and international security. I study political violence, peace processes, political behavior, and the Women, Peace and Security agenda using quantitative methods and cross-national data. My work focuses on understanding how conflict shapes political institutions, democratic governance, and post-conflict peacebuilding.

Current Research

Violence, Legitimacy, and Bargaining
Examining how wartime sexual violence influences the inclusion of gender provisions in comprehensive peace agreements.

Women's Civil Society and Peace Negotiations
Investigating how women's civil society participation shapes gender-inclusive peace agreements and peace negotiations.

Identity, Revolution, and Regime Survival in Iran
Collaborative book project examining nationalism, identity, revolutionary movements, and regime survival in contemporary Iran.
